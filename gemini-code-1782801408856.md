# 🇹🇼 CYCU Summer Camp 2026: Weekend Exploration Itinerary

Hey everyone! Since we are only allowed to explore during the weekends between our arrival (**July 31**) and departure (**August 20**), here is a curated weekend-by-weekend travel guide. This itinerary is specifically packed with awesome hiking trails, massive night markets, beautiful nature parks, and great food spots that are easily accessible from our dormitory at **Chung Yuan Christian University (CYCU)**.

---

## 📅 Quick Calendar Overview
*   **Weekend 1:** August 1 (Sat) & August 2 (Sun) – *Local Nature, City Views & Night Market Crawls*
*   **Weekend 2:** August 8 (Sat) & August 9 (Sun) – *Historic Baroque Streets & Taipei's Iconic Skyline Hike*
*   **Weekend 3:** August 15 (Sat) & August 16 (Sun) – *Deep Cedar Forest Trekking & Pre-Departure Hangout*

---

## 🗓️ WEEKEND 1: Local Parks, Stunning Sunsets & Street Food

### 🏞️ Saturday, August 1: Hutoushan Hiking & Panoramic Sunset
*   **Daytime Activity:** Head over to **Hutoushan Park** (Tiger Mountain). It's a massive urban forest park featuring great networks of shaded walking trails, serene lotus ponds, and pavilions. It's the perfect easy-to-moderate hike to get our legs moving.
*   **Sunset Spot:** Right next to it is the **Hutoushan Environmental Park**. It sits on high ground and is widely known by locals as the absolute best place in Taoyuan to sit back, watch the sunset, and enjoy panoramic night views of the city terrace.
*   **Dinner Spot:** Wrap up the night at **Taoyuan Tourist Night Market** to grab some crispy Vietnamese shrimp pancakes, pan-fried pork dumplings, and refreshing iced desserts.

### 🍢 Sunday, August 2: Ultimate Zhongli Night Market Crawl
*   **Daytime Activity:** Keep it casual during the day. We can walk through **Juguang Environmental Protection Park** or check out the nearby **Zhongyuanpitang Ecological Park** to enjoy some lakeside fresh air.
*   **Dinner Spot:** Hit up the famous **Zhongli Night Market**. It spans over 700 meters and is packed with hundreds of stalls. 
    *   *Must-Try Foods:* Noodles with savory pork intestines, local luwei (braised custom platters), clay-oven sesame rolls, and fresh, crispy sweet potato balls.

---

## 🗓️ WEEKEND 2: Historical Day Trip & Taipei's Postcard View

### 🧱 Saturday, August 8: Old-World Charm at Daxi Old Street
*   **Daytime Activity:** Take a trip to **Daxi Old Street**. The area is famous for its gorgeous Japanese-colonial era Baroque facades, historic storefronts, and cultural vibes. It feels like stepping back in time.
*   **Green Spaces:** Take a relaxed walk through **Daxi Zhongzheng Park**, which sits right on the cliffside overlooking the river valley below.
*   **Food Highlights:** You absolutely have to try the legendary **Daxi Dried Tofu (Dougan)**, traditional savory brown rice cakes, and smooth, handmade aiyu jelly drinks.

### 🧗 Sunday, August 9: Elephant Mountain Skyline Hike (Taipei)
*   **The Main Event:** Take a train/MRT into Taipei to conquer the **Elephant Mountain Trail (Xiangshan)**. This is the trail that gives you that famous, postcard-perfect panoramic view of Taipei 101 and the towering capital skyline.
*   **Trail Info:** It's a short but intense vertical climb up steep stone steps surrounded by edge-to-edge green foliage. 
*   **Timing Tip:** We'll start climbing in the late afternoon so we can catch the golden hour sunset and watch Taipei's lights flicker on.
*   **Post-Hike Dinner:** Head down to **Raohe Street Night Market** or **Tonghua Night Market** right after for iconic black pepper pork buns and shaved ice.

---

## 🗓️ WEEKEND 3: Wilderness Forest Trekking & Shopping

### 🌲 Saturday, August 15: Deep Wilderness at Dongyanshan
*   **Daytime Activity:** Escape the summer heat by heading deep into **Dongyanshan National Forest Recreation Area** (East Eye Mountain). This place is gorgeous—it features massive, towering cedar forests, ancient tree fossils, misty walking tracks, and hidden waterfalls.
*   **The Summit:** Pushing up to the summit ridge rewards you with an incredible view looking all the way across the mountains into the Taipei Basin on a clear day.
*   **Pro-Tip:** Make sure to pack your own trail snacks and plenty of water, as options deep inside the mountain park are limited!

### 🛍️ Sunday, August 16: Souvenirs, Aquarium & Chill Out
*   **Daytime Activity:** Relaxed group hangout before our final project presentations and check-out week. We can hit up the giant **Gloria Outlets** for shopping, explore the stunning **Xpark Aquarium** (right next to Taoyuan HSR Station), or browse around the **Metrowalk Shopping District** to pick up unique Taiwan snacks and souvenirs for family and friends.
